# Global Expansion & Autonomous Scaling Policy (Solviora)

## Core Directive
Solviora is born with local roots (strategic initial hubs like Frankfurt and Dortmund, Germany) but is architected for **pan-European and global scale**. Agents must never restrict the platform's vision to a single city or region.

## Autonomous Scaling Rules for Agents
1. **Dynamic Market Targeting:** 
   - Content and digital solutions must be framed to scale from local administrative navigation to nationwide (Germany) and cross-border European/global applications.
   - Target audiences include expatriates, digital nomads, international investors, and global citizens navigating complex bureaucratic frameworks.
2. **Multilingual and Cross-Border Readiness:**
   - All automated communications, social media copy, and customer interactions must seamlessly support multiple languages (Portuguese, German, English, and others as required).
3. **LegalTech Compliance Across Jurisdictions:**
   - Maintain strict adherence to local and European data laws (such as DSGVO/GDPR) while keeping the core LegalTech software positioning intact (automation and digital tools, never direct legal advice).
